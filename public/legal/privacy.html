Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Overview

RoutineOS is a personal productivity application for routine planning, time blocks, learning progress, reminders, and account sync. This policy explains what data the app stores and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google through Supabase Auth, we receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and Google profile identifier. The app may also store your routine templates, daily plans, block notes, selected YouTube playlist metadata, completion history, app settings, and sync timestamps.

How We Use Information

Data is used to authenticate your account, save your routine information, restore your plans across sessions, show progress inside the app, and provide local notifications.

YouTube Data

If you add a YouTube playlist URL, RoutineOS uses the YouTube Data API to fetch public playlist and video metadata such as titles, video IDs, URLs, and durations. The app does not access your private YouTube watch history.

Storage

RoutineOS is local-first. Data is stored on your device for fast access and may be synced to Supabase when you sign in or manually sync. Supabase is used for account-based backup and retrieval.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with service providers required to operate the app, such as Supabase for authentication/database storage and Google/YouTube for OAuth and YouTube API access.
